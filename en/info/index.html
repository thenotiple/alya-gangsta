Info

Everything worth knowing before you come in.

Before the session

  1. Sleep well and eat a proper meal beforehand.
  2. No alcohol the day before and on the day.
  3. Moisturise the area in the days before; avoid sunburn.
  4. Wear comfortable clothes that give easy access to the spot.
  5. Bring a valid ID — tattoos are for 18+ only.

Aftercare

  1. Keep the film on as advised, then wash gently with lukewarm water and mild soap.
  2. Apply a thin layer of aftercare cream 2–3 times a day.
  3. No sauna, swimming pool, sea or solarium for 2–3 weeks.
  4. Don’t scratch or pick the scabs; protect from direct sun.

Deposit & termin

Sending a request does not book the termin yet. I will contact you first; the termin is confirmed after a deposit of 50 €. The deposit is non-refundable and is deducted from the final price.
